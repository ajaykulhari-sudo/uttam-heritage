Four rooms. One easy booking.

01 — Executive Room

The Executive Room

The finest room in the house. A double bed set against a panelled feature wall, brass pendant lamps either side, cove lighting overhead, and a polished marble-look floor that runs the length of the room.

The bathroom is finished floor to ceiling in marble-effect tile, with a white vessel basin on a stone vanity. A smart TV, body wash and shampoo, and a dental kit are laid out for you.
