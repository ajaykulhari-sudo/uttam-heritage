Welcome

A heritage name, a modern finish.

Saharanpur is a city people come to for work, for family, for a wedding, or on the way up to the hills — and most of them want the same thing at the end of the day: a clean, quiet room they can find easily and park outside.

That's what Uttam Heritage is built for. The rooms are finished in polished marble-look floors and dark walnut panelling, lit warm, and made up fresh every day. The front desk is staffed through the night, the lift runs to every floor, and the power backup means the lights stay on when the city's don't.

The Experience

What's here.

No pool, no spa — just the things that make a city stay easy, done properly. These are the ones guests actually ask about.

Front Desk, 24×7

Reception is staffed round the clock, so a late train or an early flight is never a problem. Early check-in and late check-out on request.

Free Parking & Lift

Park free at the hotel and take the lift up with your bags. Right on Ambala Road, opposite V-Mart — easy to find, even after dark.

Room Service

An in-house kitchen and room service to the door. Add breakfast or a meal plan when you book, and it's taken care of.

WiFi & Power Backup

WiFi across the hotel, air conditioning in the A/C rooms, and full power backup — so calls, laptops and the geyser keep working.

Couples & Families

Couple friendly for guests over 18 with valid ID. Children under five stay free, and an extra mattress can be added on request.

Safe & Looked After

CCTV, smoke detectors, fire extinguishers and first aid at the desk. Daily housekeeping and a laundry service keep the rest in order.
