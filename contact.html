Getting Here

Ask for the Clock Tower.

The hotel is on Ambala Road, opposite V-Mart, by Saharanpur's Ghanta Ghar. From Saharanpur railway station it's a short auto or taxi ride — tell the driver "Ghanta Ghar, Ambala Road" and you're there. Free parking is at the hotel if you're driving in. Tap any landmark for turn-by-turn directions.
